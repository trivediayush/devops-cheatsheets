# 👤 About

**DevOps Cheatsheets** — a practical, interview-depth reference for DevOps, SRE, and platform engineering work. Built to be the place you look things up instead of hunting through tutorials.

## Why this exists

Most cheatsheets are either too shallow (just commands) or too dense (man-page walls). This project aims for the middle: **commands + context + the internals that explain why things break**, organized by tool and by the job you're doing — daily ops, production debugging, or interview prep.

## What's inside

- **120+ cheatsheets across 20 tracks** covering Platform Engineering, SRE & Reliability, Kubernetes, Cloud, CI/CD, IaC, Observability, Security, DevSecOps, Networking, Linux, AI/GPU Infrastructure, and Programming
- **Daily ops sheets** — the commands you actually type every day
- **Internals & architecture** — control-plane flows, kernel internals, packet walks, with diagrams
- **Edge scenarios & war-room playbooks** — production outage cascades, Kubernetes/Cloud/Security edge cases with visual diagrams & answers, and air-gapped deploys
- **Interview prep** — tiered Q&A from fresher to senior, Platform & SRE System Design, plus rapid-fire revision
